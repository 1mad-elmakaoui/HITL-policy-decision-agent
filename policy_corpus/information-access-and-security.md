## Section 1. Access Principle

Access to company systems and data is granted on the principle of least
privilege: an individual receives the minimum access required to perform their
role, and no more. Access is granted to a named individual and is not shared.
Shared or generic accounts are prohibited except for approved service accounts
with a registered owner.

## Section 2. Requesting and Approving Access

Access requests are raised through the access management system and require
approval from the data owner for the system concerned. Access to systems
classified Restricted additionally requires Information Security approval.
Standing approvals are not permitted; every grant carries an expiry date of no
more than twelve months.

## Section 3. Access Review

Every system owner conducts a quarterly access review and certifies that each
retained grant remains necessary. Grants not certified within thirty days of the
review date are revoked automatically.

## Section 4. Revocation on Role Change or Exit

Access is revoked on the employee's last working day, or immediately upon
suspension where the suspension relates to alleged misconduct involving company
systems or data. On an internal role change, access associated with the previous
role is revoked within five working days unless re-approved for the new role.

Revocation of access is an administrative security control. It is not a
disciplinary sanction and does not require the disciplinary approvals in the
Employment Conduct and Discipline Policy. Where revocation is applied to an
employee under investigation, People Operations must be informed on the same
day so that the employment consequences are handled under the correct process.

## Section 5. Monitoring of Employee Activity

Routine, aggregate monitoring of system usage for security purposes is
permitted and is disclosed to employees at onboarding. Targeted monitoring of an
identified individual's activity is a distinct measure and requires prior
approval from Legal and a People Operations partner, a documented justification,
and a defined time limit. Covert monitoring without that approval is prohibited.

Material obtained through monitoring conducted outside this section may not be
relied upon in a disciplinary process.

## Section 6. Data Classification

Data is classified Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential
and Restricted data may not be copied to personal devices, personal cloud
storage or personal email. Restricted data may not leave the production
environment without an approved data export request.

## Section 7. Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident, including a lost device, a suspected phishing
compromise, or accidental disclosure, must be reported to Information Security
within twenty-four hours of discovery. Good-faith reporting of an incident,
including one the reporter caused, is not itself a disciplinary matter.

## Section 8. Third-Party and Contractor Access

Contractor access is sponsored by a named employee, is time-limited to the
engagement, and is reviewed monthly. Third-party organisations receive access
only under an executed data processing agreement reviewed by Legal.
