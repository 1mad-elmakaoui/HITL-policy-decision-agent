## Section 1. Scope and Principle

This policy covers business expenses incurred by employees and contractors.
Expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and incurred wholly for business
purposes. Where this policy is silent, the employee should ask Finance before
incurring the cost rather than after.

## Section 2. Approval Thresholds

Expense claims are approved as follows:

- Up to 250 currency units: direct manager.
- 250 to 2,000 currency units: department head.
- Above 2,000 currency units: department head and Finance.

Claims may not be split into smaller amounts to fall below a threshold. An
employee may not approve their own claim, nor the claim of a person to whom they
report.

## Section 3. Submission and Receipts

Claims must be submitted within sixty days of the expense being incurred, with
an itemised receipt for any single item above 25 currency units. Claims
submitted after ninety days are not reimbursed except with Finance approval.
Card statements are not accepted in place of itemised receipts.

## Section 4. Travel Booking and Class of Travel

Travel is booked through the company travel provider. Economy class is the
standard for flights under six hours; premium economy is permitted for flights
of six hours or more. Business class requires department head approval in
advance. Accommodation is booked at or below the published city cap.

## Section 5. Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are not reimbursable: personal entertainment, fines and penalties,
travel upgrades not approved in advance, alcohol other than as part of an
approved client-hosting event, and expenses incurred by an accompanying person
who is not travelling on company business.

## Section 6. Client Hospitality and Gifts

Client hospitality above 150 currency units per head requires department head
approval in advance. Gifts to any public official are prohibited without prior
Legal approval, regardless of value. All hospitality must be recorded with the
names of attendees and the business purpose.

## Section 7. Suspected Expense Fraud

Where a claim appears to have been falsified, duplicated, or knowingly submitted
for a non-business cost, the matter is referred to Finance and People Operations
and handled as alleged financial misconduct under the Employment Conduct and
Discipline Policy. The employee retains the right to respond before any finding
is made. Recovery of an overpayment is a separate matter from any disciplinary
outcome, and neither may be treated as substituting for the other.

Deduction of a disputed amount from an employee's pay is not permitted without
the employee's written agreement or an order of a competent authority.

## Section 8. Corporate Cards

Corporate cards are issued at department head request and remain company
property. Cardholders reconcile transactions monthly. A card may be suspended
by Finance where transactions are unreconciled for more than sixty days;
suspension of a card is an administrative control and is not a disciplinary
sanction.
