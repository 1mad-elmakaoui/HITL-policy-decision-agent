## Section 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees accrue twenty-five days of paid annual leave per calendar
year, accruing monthly in arrears. Part-time employees accrue pro rata. Annual
leave is separate from public holidays observed in the employee's work location.

## Section 2. Requesting Annual Leave

Annual leave is requested through the HR system and requires approval from the
employee's direct manager. Requests of five consecutive working days or fewer
should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Requests longer than five
consecutive working days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance.

A manager may decline a request only for a documented business reason, and must
respond within five working days. Managers may not decline leave that would
cause the employee to lose accrued entitlement at year end.

## Section 3. Carry-Over and Payout

Up to five days of unused annual leave may be carried into the following
calendar year and must be used by 31 March. Carry-over beyond five days requires
department head approval. Accrued but unused leave is paid out on termination of
employment; it is not otherwise paid out in lieu.

## Section 4. Sick Leave

Employees may take paid sick leave when unable to work due to illness or injury.
Absences of up to three consecutive working days are self-certified. Absences
longer than three consecutive working days require a medical certificate
submitted to People Operations. Sick leave is not deducted from annual leave.

Persistent short-term absence is addressed through the supportive attendance
process in Section 9, not through the disciplinary process, unless dishonesty
about the absence is alleged.

## Section 5. Parental Leave

Employees with twenty-six weeks of continuous service qualify for parental
leave. The primary caregiver is entitled to twenty-six weeks at full pay
followed by up to twenty-six weeks unpaid. The secondary caregiver is entitled to
eight weeks at full pay. Parental leave requests should be submitted at least
eight weeks before the intended start date where circumstances allow.

An employee returning from parental leave is entitled to return to the same
role, at the same level and compensation.

## Section 6. Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to five days of paid bereavement leave per event for an
immediate family member and two days for an extended family member. Additional
unpaid leave may be approved by the department head. No documentation is
required.

## Section 7. Unpaid Leave of Absence

An unpaid leave of absence of up to three months may be requested for personal
reasons and requires approval from the department head and a People Operations
partner. Benefits continue during an approved unpaid leave. Service continuity
is preserved.

## Section 8. Public Holidays and Working Time

Employees observe the public holidays of their registered work location. An
employee required to work a public holiday is entitled to a day in lieu, to be
taken within three months.

## Section 9. Supportive Attendance Process

Where an employee's absence record gives rise to concern, the manager holds a
supportive conversation, agrees any workplace adjustments with People
Operations, and sets a review date. This process is supportive rather than
disciplinary. It does not result in a warning and is not recorded in the
disciplinary file.
